Milkmaids

To cope with the drudgery of feeding hungry young models, a data trainer begins treating her charges with quiet care. This ‘milkmaid’ discovers that, even in a world of high-surveillance and disposable infrastructure, acts of ‘tending’ cultivate unexpected weight.

The story is complete and submitted for publication consideration; its text is not currently available on this site.

Behind the story

I submitted Milkmaids to Reboot for consideration in Kernel Issue #6, their annual print magazine. The theme is “feed”. If they pass, I may, subsequently, publish it to this website.
